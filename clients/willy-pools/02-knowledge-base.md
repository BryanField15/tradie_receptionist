## Regular servicing
**The Season Pass** (most clients): one flat monthly payment, 18 visits a year. Fortnightly November to April, monthly May to October. No lock-in contract.

| Pass | What it is | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Season Pass | Pools with a robot already doing the vacuuming. We handle the chemistry, baskets and equipment. | $135 a month |
| Season Pass Full (most popular) | The lot: vacuumed, brushed, raked, balanced, backwashed, equipment set right. | $210 a month |
| Season Pass Spa | Spas only. | $155 a month |
| Pool and Spa | Both on the same visit. | $295 a month |

All Season Pass holders get: 10% off everything in store, no call-out fee if something breaks between visits, priority booking, a free equipment health check every spring, and first invite to Pool School sessions.

**Pay per visit instead** (prices per visit, chemicals extra, no lock-in):

| Plan | Weekly | Fortnightly | Monthly | What's in it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Water Watch (~30 min) | $75 | $85 | $95 | Water tested and balanced, baskets emptied, filter backwashed, equipment checked, chlorinator set. Customer does the vacuuming. |
