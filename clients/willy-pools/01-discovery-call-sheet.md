# Willy Pools: 10-minute discovery call

Goal: leave the call with everything needed to build the demo agent within 3 days.
Fill answers straight into `02-knowledge-base.md` (every `[ASK WILLY]` there maps to a question here).

Tip: ask if you can record the call (phone voice memo) so you can lift his exact wording for answers. Callers trust an agent that sounds like the owner.

---

## 1. Calls today (2 min)
1. Which number do customers ring: your mobile, a landline, or both?
2. Who answers when you're on a job or driving? Anyone in the store?
3. In peak (Oct to Mar), roughly how many calls a day, and how many do you miss?
4. When you miss one, what happens now? Do you call back? How long does that usually take?
5. Which carrier and plan is the mobile on? (Needed to set "forward when busy / unanswered"; check that forwarding to another AU number is included.)

## 2. The questions people ask (3 min)
6. What are the top 10 questions people ask? For each, what do you normally say?
   Prompt him if he stalls: hours, address/parking, brands, water testing, price of servicing, suburbs, "my pool is green", "pump won't start", robot cleaner repairs, warranty, chemicals in stock, gift vouchers, payment methods.
7. Store hours, summer and winter. When does the switch happen? Public holidays?
8. Free water test: how much water to bring, in what (bottle size, how deep to take it), how long it takes, any days it isn't available?
9. Parking and how to find the store (is 11/10 Literary Way in a unit complex? which door?)

## 3. Services and prices (3 min)
10. Regular service: what's included in a visit? How often (weekly, fortnightly, monthly)? Price, "from $150/month plus chemicals" — is that right to say on the phone?
11. One-off clean / green-pool recovery: do you do it, and what can the AI say about price ("from $X" or "quote on inspection")?
12. Repairs and equipment: what do you fix and install (pumps, filters, chlorinators, heaters, robots)? Call-out fee?
13. Brands you stock or service (Maytronics/Dolphin, others?). Anything you **don't** touch?
14. Suburbs covered. Anything just outside the list you'd still take, or never take?
15. How fast can you usually get to a new client or a breakdown in peak?

## 4. What the AI should do (1 min)
16. Which callers are "drop everything" for you? (e.g. equipment sales, commercial pools, existing clients with a broken pump)
17. Callback promise: what can we honestly promise? ("within 2 business hours"? "same day"?)
18. Anything the AI must **never** say or promise? (prices, dates, chemical dosing advice?)
19. Are you OK with an AI voice answering, or would you rather start with text-back only? (Offer to let him ring the demo first.)

## 5. Systems and admin (1 min)
20. Liqua: does it have an online booking link or customer portal we could text to callers?
21. Store POS: is it Lightspeed? Which plan (X-Series? Plus?)
22. Where should summaries go: which mobile for SMS, which email?
23. Website: who owns the Wix login and the domain? Google Business Profile access?

## Close the call
- "I'll have a test number you can ring yourself by [day]. Ring it, try to trip it up, and tell me what it gets wrong."
- Book the 15-minute demo slot now.
- Don't quote the monthly price as final on this call.
